Why Nutley homes need a fast plumber

Nutley is a township of older one- and two-family homes, with many basements and a lot of mature trees on its streets. Those two facts explain most of our calls: roots in old sewer laterals and aging water heaters in basement utility corners.

The Third River runs through Nutley before it reaches the Passaic, and heavy rain tests basement drains and sump pumps across the township.

Winter calls in Nutley

Cold snaps bring frozen outdoor faucets and split lines in unheated spaces. If you see ice on a pipe, open the faucet, warm the area gently and close the main if you see a crack. Our line is open 24/7 for freeze emergencies.

What Nutley homeowners can do before winter

Cold snaps hit older homes hardest.

  • Insulate pipes along exterior walls and in unheated basements.
  • Disconnect hoses and drain outdoor faucets.
  • Test the sump pump and its discharge line.
  • Know where the main shutoff is, and test that it turns.
  • Flush the water heater to clear sediment.
