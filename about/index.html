Who we are

Belleville 24/7 Plumbing is a plumbing service based at 135 Main St, Belleville, NJ 07109. We take emergency and scheduled plumbing calls for Belleville and the towns around it, and our line answers 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

We built the business around a simple idea. When water is on the floor or a drain is backing up, you want a person on the phone, a clear price and a plumber who explains what he finds. We try to do exactly that.

How we work

Every visit starts with a diagnosis. We tell you what is wrong, what the options are and what each costs before we start. If a cheaper fix will do, we say so.

We tell you when a job belongs to someone else. Burner and combustion work on a boiler goes to a heating contractor, and a leak in the public main goes to the township at 973-450-3300.

After-hours dispatch costs more than a weekday visit. We say that on the phone, every time.

What we handle

Our work runs from emergencies to planned projects. On the emergency side that means sewer and drain backups, burst and frozen pipes, water heater failures, flooded basements from failed sump pumps and leaks inside walls. On the planned side it means water heater and tankless installs, repiping, fixture installs, camera inspections and basement drainage.

Commercial customers call us too. Restaurants, shops and small offices in Belleville and the towns around it need a plumber who will come after closing time and finish before opening.

Every service has its own page with plain-language detail on what the problem is, what the options are and what changes the price. Start at the services page and choose the one that fits.

Belleville, the place we work

Belleville is a township of older houses, two-family homes and small businesses between the Second River and the Passaic River. Silver Lake, Belwood, Big Tree and Soho are the neighborhoods people name when they call. We know how these houses were built, which is why we ask about basements, stacks and shutoffs early in the call.

The Belleville Water Department at 973-450-3414 handles meters and billing, and the township office at 973-450-3300 answers permit questions. We point you to them whenever the answer is theirs to give, and we do not guess about rules we do not control.

Belleville's 2023 water quality report listed hardness at 48.7 ppm, about 2.9 grains per gallon, which is soft by the USGS scale. We use that fact when we advise on water heaters, softeners and scale, so the advice fits the water you actually have.

What you can expect when you call

A person answers. We ask what you see, hear or smell, where the problem is, and whether water is still flowing. We tell you what to close or switch off, and we give an arrival window.

At the house, the plumber finds the cause first. You get the price and the options before work starts, and the price does not change unless conditions turn out to differ from what we found, in which case we stop and talk to you.

When the work is done, we test it, explain what we did and leave you with simple steps to prevent a repeat.

How we price a job

We quote the work you need, not the work that pays best. The price covers the labor, the parts and the cleanup, and it is given before we start. After-hours dispatch costs more than a weekday visit, and the difference is stated on the phone.

If we find something unexpected, such as a rotted subfloor or a corroded shutoff, we stop, show you, and explain what it adds. You decide whether to go ahead. We do not add work you did not approve.

Ask us for the reasoning behind any line of a quote. A plumber who cannot explain a charge should not be charging it.

Licensing

New Jersey requires plumbing to be performed by a master plumber, an authorized plumbing contractor or their employees. The plumbing subcode adopts the 2021 National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated through N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15.

You can ask any plumber for the license number on the invoice and check it with the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers. We welcome that.
