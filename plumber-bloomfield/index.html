Bloomfield has a dense mix of multi-family houses, apartment buildings and small commercial blocks around Bloomfield Center. That means more shared drains and shared water heaters than in a typical suburb, and more calls where one unit's problem reaches another.

Why a fast response matters in apartment buildings

In a multi-unit building, a slow leak above a ceiling damages every unit below it. The first hour matters. We tell the caller how to close the shutoff for the unit and where the building main is.

When a landlord is not reachable, the tenant can still call. We document what we find and what we did, so the owner has a record.

Older commercial blocks around Bloomfield Center

Small shops and restaurants share walls and drains with apartments above. A grease backup from a kitchen can reach the upstairs bath. Regular trap cleaning and a camera check of the shared lateral prevent most of these calls.

Code we work to in Bloomfield

New Jersey's plumbing subcode adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated, 2021 edition, through N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15. In multi-unit buildings that includes shutoff valves for each unit and accessible cleanouts, which also make the next emergency easier to handle.
