Disposal emergency: what to do right now

If water is leaking or the sink is backing up, these steps protect the kitchen.

  • Switch off the disposal and the wall switch, and never put a hand inside.
  • Close the shutoff valves under the sink if water is leaking.
  • Do not pour drain cleaner into a jammed unit.
  • Move items out of the cabinet and mop standing water.
  • Switch off the dishwasher if it drains into the disposal.

Leaks from the body mean the unit has reached the end of its life. Repair is rarely worth it.

When the sink is backed up, not just the disposal

If the sink fills with water when the disposal runs, the clog may be past the disposal. Grease and starchy food are the usual cause, and the blockage may reach into the kitchen branch line.

We clear the line with a cable or jetter, then check how the disposal and the dishwasher drain tie in. See drain cleaning for the broader fix.

What not to put down a disposal

Most jams come from the same items.

  • Grease, oil and fat.
  • Coffee grounds in large amounts.
  • Potato peels, pasta and rice, which swell.
  • Fibrous items such as celery, corn husks and onion skins.
  • Bones, fruit pits and hard shells.
  • Non-food items like glass, metal and plastic.

Run cold water for a short time before and after use. It moves waste along.

Repair or replace

A jam is usually a repair. A motor that has burned out, a leaking body or a unit that is more than a decade old is normally a replacement.

We size the new unit to the household and the sink, check the dishwasher connection and add a new mounting kit and drain gasket. Installation includes testing for leaks and running the unit with water.

Electrical and plumbing rules

New Jersey's plumbing subcode adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated, 2021 edition, through N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15. Disposal wiring falls under the National Electrical Code (NEC) instead. A plug-in unit on a working outlet is a plumbing job. A new outlet, switch or circuit is electrical work, and we tell you when a licensed electrician must do it.
