North Arlington's housing and plumbing

North Arlington is a compact borough of mostly one- and two-family houses, many built decades ago, on small lots with mature street trees. That combination means older pipes and roots in the sewer laterals.

Because the lots are small, a sewer line often runs under a driveway or a neighbor's property line, so repair planning needs care.

Small lots, shared lines and tight access

On a small lot, a sewer lateral can run close to a neighbor's line or under a driveway. A camera locates the pipe and its depth before anyone digs, which avoids damage to the wrong line.

When access is tight, we use compact tools and plan the work around parked cars and shared driveways. We tell you the plan and the time window before we start.

Keeping an older home's plumbing reliable

A few small habits go a long way. Test the main shutoff each spring, replace braided supply lines every several years, and flush the water heater yearly. Keep the cleanout reachable and capped.

If you plan to sell, a camera inspection of the lateral gives you facts before a buyer's inspector asks.
